Home & Horizons

Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026

This policy covers Home and Horizons Uploader, a private tool the owner of the Home & Horizons YouTube channel uses to upload and schedule videos to that channel.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API with one permission: uploading videos to the channel owner's own YouTube account. It does not read, collect, or store information about viewers, subscribers, or any other YouTube users.

What is stored, and where

The sign-in token that lets the app upload is stored only on the channel owner's personal computer. The app keeps a local record of videos it has uploaded (titles, video IDs, and schedule times). Nothing is sent to any other server, sold, or shared with third parties.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings. Revoking access stops all uploads; the locally stored token can then be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
